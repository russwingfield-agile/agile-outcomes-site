Services

Six ways to work together.

Start small with a health check, or bring me in to lead. Each engagement is scoped up front, with clear deliverables and a defined finish. Pick the one that sounds most like your situation.

Diagnose · 2–3 weeks

Delivery Health Check

A short, evidence-led look at how work really flows from idea to release and what gets in the way. You finish with a shared picture and a plan, not a slide deck of opinions.

What you get

  • A baseline of flow metrics: lead time, deployment frequency and backlog health
  • A map of how work moves and where it waits
  • A review of planning, prioritisation and team practices
  • A prioritised plan for the first 90 days
  • A readout for your leadership team

How it runs

Length
2–3 weeks
Format
Alongside your teams, on site or remote
I need from you
Access to your work-tracking tools and a handful of conversations with leaders and teams
  • One shared, evidence-based picture
  • The first three changes worth making
Where this has worked · recruitment platform

Set a baseline of delivery metrics across 11 teams in three countries, giving leaders their first consistent view of where to improve.

Plan · 4–6 weeks

Outcomes and Forecasting

I help leaders turn strategy into outcomes that teams can plan against, and replace fixed-date promises with forecasts that show a realistic range.

What you get

  • Outcome-based goals and OKRs linked from company level to teams
  • A roadmap built on forecast ranges instead of single dates
  • A prioritisation framework your Product Owners can run themselves
  • A quarterly planning cycle, run once with you and then handed over

How it runs

Length
4–6 weeks
Format
Workshops with leadership and Product, plus coaching through your first planning cycle
I need from you
A senior sponsor, your Product Owners, and access to the current roadmap and delivery data
  • Priorities leaders can explain
  • Plans with honest ranges
Where this has worked · global investment bank, as a consultant

Coached senior leaders to turn strategy into goals their delivery teams could measure and act on, with prioritisation and planning at the centre of the portfolio. At a recruitment platform, I extended quarterly planning so delivery plans sat alongside company OKRs.

  • Working agreements on using metrics to improve, not to rank teams
  • A steering rhythm where data informs scope, staffing and milestones

How it runs

4–8 weeks
Format

I need from you
A delivery lead or analyst to pair with, and access to your tracking data

  • Metrics that teams trust

Guided c-suite stakeholders through establishing delivery metrics in their own organisations, while client NPS rose from −50 to +40. At a data publisher, a first release burn-up chart changed decisions on scope and milestones.

How I work

Agile is the means. The outcome is the point.

  • Start from the data

    Before I recommend anything, I baseline how work really flows: lead time, deployment frequency and backlog health. Opinions come second.

  • Change the mindset first

    Frameworks are the easy part. Transformations stick when the way people think about value, ownership and improvement shifts.

  • Work at every level

    From the c-suite to Product Owners and engineers. Strategy that teams cannot act on fails, and so do practices that leaders cannot see.

  • Leave capability behind

    I coach, train and mentor so your people can run it without me. That is servant leadership applied to a consultancy.

About

I have worked on this from every seat.

I started in 2001 as a programmer, so I know what delivery feels like from inside the code. Over the next two decades I moved through Scrum Master, development manager and Head of Development, then into leading an agile practice, coaching, and consulting at enterprise scale.

That path shapes how I work. I care about engineering practice as much as ceremonies, I treat data as the starting point for any change, and I aim to leave teams able to run without me. I am a motivational leader who likes to lead by example.

Outside work I am a club runner, usually entering a 10k or half marathon each year, and I have played and coached American football.
