About

Eighteen years leading delivery, from team to enterprise.

Agile Outcomes is the way Russ Wingfield thinks about delivery. He has led it for eighteen years at every level of the four areas below: as a Scrum Master and development manager, Head of Development leading Agile transformation, head of an agile practice, Head of Agile Delivery at a large investment bank and, most recently, Senior Delivery Lead at Revantage, a Blackstone company. Before that he spent seven years as a programmer, so he knows what delivery looks like from inside the code.

Most recently he led AI adoption across Technology, delivering a bespoke AI productivity tool to c.2,000 users across 20 Blackstone portfolio companies and building Cursor AI applications integrated with Jira and GitLab to automate delivery work.

That mix shapes how Agile Outcomes works. Engineering practice matters as much as ceremonies, data is the starting point for any change, and the aim is to leave teams able to run without outside help.

Outside work, Russ is a regular ultra runner, currently aiming for 100 miles in his next backyard ultra, and has played and coached American football.

Case studies

The problem, what was done, and what changed.

Four short examples from earlier work, with employers left out. More context is available in conversation.

Financial services group · about 40 engineers Delivery leadership

2× release frequency

The problem
A monthly release cycle, with teams not organised for steady flow.
What was done
Formed stable cross-functional teams on Kanban and Lean principles, and championed continuous integration, pairing and test-driven development.
The result
Releases went from monthly to fortnightly while throughput increased, and annual staff turnover stayed under 2.5%.

Financial services group · programme delivery Programme delivery

8 new partner schemes in one year

The problem
Only two new partner schemes had been delivered the year before, across several business partners.
What was done
Took on governance and management of technology programme delivery across the partners.
The result
Eight new affinity partner schemes implemented in a single year, up from two.

Recruitment platform · three countries Agile coaching

11 delivery teams on one metrics baseline

The problem
The Agile practice needed re-establishing across a department in London, Belarus and Vietnam, and quarterly planning set objectives without delivery plans.
What was done
Led a team of Scrum Masters, set a baseline of lead time, deployment frequency and backlog health, added delivery plans to OKR planning, and ran tailored Scrum training.
The result
One shared set of delivery metrics across all 11 teams, a step change in Agile maturity, and company goals that were far more visible in planning.

Pharma business intelligence · new API team Team delivery

1st release burn-up chart, with a predictive view of delivery

The problem
A new in-house team was building brand new APIs for clinical trial and drug data, and the business had no predictive view of when its workstreams would deliver.
What was done
Worked as the team’s Scrum Master, coached new teams in user story mapping, vertical story slicing and acceptance criteria, and set up the first release burn-up chart for the business unit.
The result
A predictive view of deliveries that triggered key decisions on scope and milestones.
