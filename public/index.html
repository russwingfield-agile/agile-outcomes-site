Case studies

The problem, what I did, and what changed.

Four short examples from earlier roles, with employers left out. I am happy to share more context in conversation.

Financial services group · about 40 engineers

2× release frequency

The problem
A monthly release cycle, with teams not organised for steady flow.
What I did
Formed stable cross-functional teams on Kanban and Lean principles, and championed continuous integration, pairing and test-driven development.
The result
Releases went from monthly to fortnightly while throughput increased, and annual staff turnover stayed under 2.5%.

Financial services group · programme delivery

8 new partner schemes in one year

The problem
Only two new partner schemes had been delivered the year before, across several business partners.
What I did
Took on governance and management of technology programme delivery across the partners.
The result
Eight new affinity partner schemes implemented in a single year, up from two.

Recruitment platform · three countries

11 delivery teams on one metrics baseline

The problem
The Agile practice needed re-establishing across a department in London, Belarus and Vietnam, and quarterly planning set objectives without delivery plans.
What I did
Led a team of Scrum Masters, set a baseline of lead time, deployment frequency and backlog health, added delivery plans to OKR planning, and ran tailored Scrum training.
The result
One shared set of delivery metrics across all 11 teams, a step change in Agile maturity, and company goals that were far more visible in planning.

Engineering-metrics software start-up

+40 NPS, up from −50

The problem
Customers needed help turning a delivery-metrics platform into real change inside their organisations, and NPS stood at −50.
What I did
Guided c-suite stakeholders through change programmes to establish metrics, using practices and frameworks I developed, and gave clients fast, knowledgeable support.
The result
NPS rose from −50 to +40.

About

Eighteen years leading delivery, from the inside.

I have led delivery for eighteen years as a development manager, Scrum Master, Head of Development, head of an agile practice and, more recently, a consultant and coach. Before that I spent seven years as a programmer, so I know what delivery looks like from inside the code.

That mix shapes how I work. I care about engineering practice as much as ceremonies, I treat data as the starting point for any change, and I aim to leave teams able to run without me.

Engagements

The work I take on.

Six kinds of engagement, from a short health check to a leader embedded in your delivery. Each one is scoped up front.

  • Delivery health check

    A baseline of how work really flows, and a plan for the first 90 days.

    2–3 weeks

  • Outcomes and forecasting

    OKRs, roadmaps built on forecast ranges, and planning that leaders can trust.

    4–6 weeks

  • Delivery metrics and insight

    A focused set of metrics and dashboards in your own tooling, and the habit of using them.

    4–8 weeks

  • Agile transformation

    New ways of working, organisation design and engineering practice, led from pilot teams to scale.

    3–12 months

  • Leadership and team coaching

    Coaching for leaders, Product Owners, Scrum Masters and teams, with tailored training.

    3–6 months

  • Interim delivery leadership

    A head of agile practice or senior delivery lead, hands on, with a handover plan.

    3–12 months
