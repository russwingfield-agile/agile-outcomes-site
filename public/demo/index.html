An idea, as it arrives

“Customers keep asking if they can export their invoices.”

What I asked back

  • What outcome is this for?
  • Who, exactly, is asking?
  • How will you know it worked?

Here’s my thinking

Outcome: unclear. I’ve assumed fewer “where is my invoice” tickets, falling by a third in 60 days.

My split: three stories, each usable on its own. I joined “choose a format” into story 1, because a picker with one format isn’t worth shipping alone.

Stories, ready for Jira or Azure DevOps

1. Download a single invoice as a PDF

As a finance admin, I want to download any past invoice as a PDF, so that I can file it without asking support.

  • The PDF totals match the invoice on screen
  • Invoices older than seven years show a clear message

StoryOutcome: fewer invoice-copy ticketsEstimate: 3

2. Download a date range as one CSV. A different need (month-end). I’d wait for story 1 to show demand.

3. Send invoices to an AP address automatically. Bigger risk (email, permissions). Held back until 1 and 2 are used.

An outcome, as stated

“Make us easier to buy from.”

What I asked back

  • So what changes when we achieve it?
  • What would you see or count?
  • Which work is meant to move it?

The so-what test

Not yet an outcome

“Easier to buy from” is a quality, not a change in what customers do. Sharper: more qualified trials turn into paying customers without a sales call.

Ways to measure it

  • LeadingMedian days from sign-up to first value moment
  • ResultSelf-serve trial-to-paid conversion rate
  • GuardrailRefunds within 30 days

Linked down to the backlog: 2 of 3 items

  • LinkedGuided first-run checklist moves days to first value
  • LinkedPricing page rewrite moves conversion
  • No linkWeekly report export moves neither. Does it belong this quarter?
