An outcome, as stated

“Make us easier to buy from.”

What I asked back

  • So what changes when we achieve it?
  • What would you see or count?
  • Which work is meant to move it?

The so-what test

Not yet an outcome

“Easier to buy from” describes a quality, not a change in what customers do. Keep asking “so what changes?” until you reach a behaviour you can count.

A sharper version: more qualified trials turn into paying customers without a sales call.

Ways to measure it

  • LeadingMedian days from sign-up to first value moment
  • ResultSelf-serve trial-to-paid conversion rate
  • GuardrailRefunds within 30 days, so conversion isn’t bought by selling the wrong thing

Linked down to the backlog

  • LinkedGuided first-run checklist moves days to first value
  • LinkedPricing page rewrite moves trial-to-paid conversion
  • No linkWeekly report export moves neither measure. Does it belong this quarter?
