An idea, as it arrives

“Customers keep asking if they can export their invoices.”

What I asked back

  • What outcome is this for?
  • Who, exactly, is asking?
  • How will you know it worked?

Here’s my thinking

Outcome: not clear yet. Fewer “where is my invoice” tickets, faster month-end for customers, or a blocker on a sale? Each leads to a different build. I’ve assumed fewer tickets.

Who for: finance admins at customer accounts, not everyday users.

How you’ll know: tickets tagged “invoice copy” fall by a third within 60 days.

My split: three stories, each cut so a customer can use it on its own. I joined “choose a format” into story 1, because a format picker with one format isn’t worth shipping alone.

Stories, ready for Jira or Azure DevOps

1. Download a single invoice as a PDF

As a finance admin, I want to download any past invoice as a PDF, so that I can file it without asking support.

  • The PDF totals match the invoice on screen
  • Invoices older than seven years show a clear message
  • The download is recorded in the audit log

StoryinvoicesOutcome: fewer invoice-copy ticketsEstimate: 3

Why this cut: the smallest thing a customer can use today, and it tests whether ticket volume really drops.

2. Download a date range of invoices as one CSV

As a finance admin, I want all invoices for a month in one file, so that I can reconcile without opening each one.

  • I can choose a start and end date
  • One row per invoice, with the same totals as the PDF
  • Ranges over 12 months are split into separate files

StoryinvoicesOutcome: fewer invoice-copy ticketsEstimate: 5

Why this cut: a different need (month-end), valuable alone, but I’d wait for story 1 to show real demand.

3. Send invoices to an accounts-payable address automatically

As a finance admin, I want new invoices sent to our AP inbox, so that nobody has to forward them.

  • I can set one address per account
  • Only account admins can change it
  • Failed sends are shown on the account page

StoryinvoicesOutcome: fewer invoice-copy ticketsEstimate: 8

Why this cut: separate value and bigger risk (email, permissions). Held back until 1 and 2 are used.
